Project structure

Fault / output writers

OTE and OTL writers for fault-like targets, with located OTU/reset evidence. v12 makes source-visible same-rung XIC/XIO/comparison paths searchable for supported outputs/actions. v13 links OTL targets to exact source-visible OTU paths and adds gate/task context without claiming the reset is currently valid or safe.

Timers & counters

Native timer/counter instructions are separated from scan-counter candidates. v10 can flag source-visible program-scoped peer PRE differences for SME review without recommending a value. v11 attaches source-visible timer .DN guards to exact state transitions when they occur on the same transition rung. v13 indexes source-visible RES call sites and their preceding gate evidence.

I/O & communication evidence

Produced/consumed mappings and explicit MSG/MESSAGE paths are indexed in search and surfaced as source-review evidence. Neither proves a live EtherNet/IP route or successful message transaction.

Motion / axis references

v12 can attach same-rung contact/comparison evidence to supported motion actions, but it does not prove the axis command is executing or that a displayed contact is a live blocker.

Discrepancies & review items

A finding is not automatically a machine defect. v10 adds same-name program-scoped peer consistency review. v11 adds reviewable sequence topology summaries and state/branch cues. v12 adds self-hold candidates and multiple source-visible permissive-path review. v13 adds source-review cues for visible OTL targets with no located OTU path, OTU paths with no visible OTL writer, and RES targets not resolved as native timers/counters. These are static review cues only; never add, force, bypass, or execute a reset from an offline finding alone.
